# Road to 1.0

Updated **2026-10-24** by OpenAI Codex (LLM), guided by anonymous human requirements and feedback. This is a release checklist, not a claim that the work is finished. Results always apply to a specific APK, device and scenario. See the [handoff](HANDOFF.md) for current allocation: only Huawei is available to this task; preserve its USB internet connection.

Current main Huawei build: **0.4.31-dev**. [Game Over UI](GAME-OVER-UI.md) and [REA/terrain findings](ORIGINAL-BEHAVIOR-REA.md) document this batch. Those checks do not close the full campaign or every compatibility gate. Do not transfer earlier APK results automatically to a newer build.

Natural campaign testing belongs to the human. Broad physical controller testing was deferred; later reported pause/input bugs are still in scope. Touch and technical regression checks remain in scope. Interrupted-import recovery now has an [implementation and tests](validation/IMPORT-RECOVERY.md); the other installation checks remain open.

### What we have

- A public Git project, reproducible OpenClaw/SDL patches, English installation/development guides, attribution and an open-source archive. The game UI supports Polish and English. Commercial game data remains separate.
- ARM64/ARMv7 APKs, physical Huawei Android 10 and Redmi Android 16 testing, plus selected Android 5/15 emulator trials. Each newer candidate needs its own evidence; this does not establish support for every phone.
- Import of the player's own REZ/ZIP, save tools, touch stick/buttons, variable jump height, configurable controller actions and persisted PL/EN selection for our screens and added captions.
- Controlled evidence for technical transitions across all 14 maps and boss encounters. This is not a natural campaign playthrough. [Evidence](validation/CAMPAIGN-1.0.md).
- Additional measurements of attacks with the hero above/below enemies, accepted inputs, animations and escape. Some trials use isolated geometry or disabled offensive AI; their limits are explicit. [Results](validation/VERTICAL-COMBAT.md).

[180 fixtures across 45 enemy prototypes](validation/VERTICAL-45-0.4.18.md) completed with a 48 px step and offensive AI states disabled. Active combat and natural stairs remain open. Historical Redmi checks covered upgrade, jumping and touch exits on Android 16 in 0.4.21; this is not a current device allocation. [Exact scope](validation/REDMI-0.4.21.md); natural campaign and final coverage remain open.

### Work required, in order

| Priority | Task | Completion condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | Complete a natural campaign playthrough | All 14 levels from the beginning to the finale, without teleports, protection, HP edits or infinite ammo. Preserve resources; exercise checkpoints, death/reload and every exit screen. Record the data version, APK, relevant settings and outcomes; do not imply that an unsupported difficulty selector changes gameplay. |
| P0 | Finish combat and terrain coverage | Remaining enemy types on actual stairs, ladders and ledges. Both sides, 16/32/48/64 px offsets, standing/crouching, ascent/descent, sword and projectiles. Separate reach limits from interrupted input and parry; establish available reactions to attacks. |
| P0 | Close boss mechanics | Verify phase gates, rejected damage, death/re-entry resets, ammo availability and the finale without assistance. Existing controlled victories do not establish balance or normal-campaign resource availability. |
| P0 | Validate player controls | Real touch. Broad physical controller testing remains deferred; reproduce specifically reported controller failures within the authorized device allocation. Menus, pause, Game Over, score, disconnect/resume, remapping, mixed controller/touch, short taps and multitouch. Keep controls clear of the HUD. |
| P0 | Finish installation, updates and progress safety | A novice installs/imports their own data using the installation guide. Updates preserve data, saves and bindings. Test low storage, cancellation, corrupt archives, interrupted import, backup and restore. Import recovery has 64 boundary checks, actual process-death tests on Huawei/Redmi and real ENOSPC on a bounded emulator filesystem; full REZ/ZIP and actual picker cancellation passed on Android 10/16. Large-file selection in DocumentsUI, external providers and save backups through that UI remain open. |
| P0 | Finish performance and stability | 60 FPS remains the optimization target. The user accepts 55 FPS in the heavy Huawei scene; this does not establish a universal measured minimum. Include dense scenes, 20–30 minute runs, memory and warm-device behavior. The twenty-minute 0.4.25 Huawei observation averaged about 58.99 FPS, minimum batch 57 FPS, longest interval 31 ms. All batches exceeded 55 FPS; this does not prove a per-frame or all-map minimum. Compare the same viewport/scene; do not reduce physics, AI or visual quality for a misleading gain. In the same-scene trial ARMv7 reached about 51–52 FPS versus ARM64 at 58–59; retain ARM64 on this Huawei. Investigate the delayed, low jump during very long frames on the Android-15 emulator. |
| P0 | Establish Android coverage | Final APK on Huawei/Redmi, 32/64 bit, minimum Android, newer systems, focus loss, system process death and recovery. The actual 16 KB Android-15 emulator passed menus, jump and a level exit on the separate 0.4.16 x86_64 build; final ARM build coverage on physical 16 KB hardware remains open. State exact devices/emulators and untested configurations. |
| P1 | Complete Polish and English UI | Translate remaining app-owned messages, help and menus; check long labels, small screens and controller navigation. Menus/help now have our own PL/EN labels and 109-item coverage in 0.4.16. Voices, backgrounds and original score-screen pictures remain data-dependent. Selecting English does not translate recorded dialogue. |
| P1 | Compare with the original game | Controlled jump, ladder, ledge, damage and boss comparisons using matching game version, data, supported settings and FPS. Classify matches, intentional differences, bugs and unknowns. Original Monolith source has not been established as available; comparing with OpenClaw is not comparing with that source. [Source provenance](validation/ORIGINAL-COMPARISON.md). |
| P1 | Prepare the release | Close blockers, publish honest known limitations, verify clean builds, licenses/NOTICE and absence of commercial/private data. Prepare signed APKs, matching sources and SHA-256 checksums; keep the signing key private. Retain LLM-work disclosure and upstream attribution. Label 1.0 only after its gates pass. Public source publication to SlopPort was authorized; this does not justify labelling an incomplete port as 1.0. |

If the agreed performance threshold or another required gate is not verified, it remains open. Do not transfer an older build's results to a newer APK or replace natural traversal with teleports. The final report should clearly separate completed work, release blockers and optional post-1.0 improvements.


## Historical milestones and limits

- **0.4.29:** [Huawei user regressions](validation/USER-REGRESSIONS-0.4.29.md) and [Redmi follow-up](validation/REDMI-0.4.29.md): pause, bonus music, Game Over and a buffered melee tap during hurt. A native change needs its own later compatibility evidence.
- **0.4.27:** [safe export/save validation](validation/READINESS-0.4.27.md), [signed ARM64/ARMv7 runtime checks](validation/RELEASE-0.4.27.md), and [45 active enemy variants](validation/ACTIVE-NORMAL-0.4.26.md). These are bounded checks, not a completed campaign or universal FPS guarantee.
- **0.4.26:** [full import and picker cancellation](validation/IMPORT-FLOW-0.4.26.md), [ladder/height checks on 0.4.25](validation/TERRAIN-HEIGHTS-0.4.25.md), and [private signing identity](RELEASE-SIGNING.md).
- **0.4.25:** [readiness](validation/READINESS-0.4.25.md), [Android 16](validation/REDMI-0.4.25.md), and [twenty-minute Huawei measurement](validation/SUSTAINED-HUAWEI-0.4.25.md): about 58.99 FPS, lowest batch 57 FPS, single interval up to 31 ms. This supports the accepted threshold in that scene; it is not a per-frame or all-level minimum.
- **2026-10-23 scope update:** the human took over campaign testing. [Real ENOSPC](validation/IMPORT-FULL-DISK.md) passed for import transactions on a bounded Android-5 emulator filesystem. That does not certify every document provider, large-file selection path or ordinary-permission interaction.
- **0.4.24:** [real-platform boarding/carrying](validation/PLATFORM-0.4.24.md), [natural checkpoint and normal menu reload](validation/NATURAL-CHECKPOINT-0.4.24.md), and [incomplete route record](validation/NATURAL-CAMPAIGN-0.4.23.md). Level 1/checkpoint 1 was reached; the full campaign remained incomplete. The lower route has a vertical elevator and was not established as a dead end.
- **0.4.23:** [powder-keg crash](validation/POWDER-KEG-0.4.23.md) found during a natural attempt, reproduced on 0.4.22, fixed and checked on Redmi Android 16/Huawei Android 10.
- **0.4.22:** [eleven configuration rejection cases](validation/CONFIG-IMPORT-0.4.22.md) passed on Android 5/10/16. [Twenty-minute Redmi 0.4.21 measurement](validation/SUSTAINED-REDMI-0.4.21.md) averaged 59.998 FPS with occasional stalls; this does not certify the Huawei threshold.
- **0.4.21:** [Redmi map starts, PL/EN, ARM64/ARMv7 and FPS](validation/REDMI-0.4.21.md), [import cleanup/clean build](validation/IMPORT-CLEANUP-0.4.21.md), and [limited active Tiger Guard checks](validation/ACTIVE-TIGER-0.4.21.md).

On 2026-10-22 the human accepted 55 FPS as the lower target in the heavy Huawei scene. 60 FPS remains an optimization goal rather than an absolute release requirement there. Report frame-time stalls and sustained measurements separately. None of these historical milestones authorizes current access to Redmi or establishes release 1.0.
