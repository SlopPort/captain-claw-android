# Reported gameplay issues — 2026-10-21

> Historical validation record. Results apply to the builds and scenarios named here, not automatically to the latest APK. See the [README](../../README.md) for current project status.

OpenAI Codex (LLM) implemented the code, diagnostics and trials below. A human
reported issues and supplied Huawei P9 Lite / LineageOS / Android 10. No
comparative Windows play session was performed. This is not a full natural clear.

## Game Over: touch

Confirmed defect: the screen contained text and handled Enter/Escape/Space, but
touching the text did nothing. The baseline reached the actual final-death
animation and remained at Game Over after a tap.

The Android fix enables tap confirmation only on this screen, forwarding it to
the active menu page's existing Enter handler. It does not send Android system
keys or require game-data reimport.

`tools/game_over_touch_smoke.py`: three actual final-life deaths and three touch
returns to menu passed. Local reports: `game-over-touch-baseline`,
`game-over-touch-fixed`. The test uses app-internal touch events, not screen hardware.

## Edges and ladders

`tools/ladder_smoke.py --idle-edges` passed top/bottom, jump and edge-fall cases.
On an isolated platform, the actor center 20, 5 and 1 pixels inside either edge
stayed stable for 1.2 s without movement. One pixel outside caused the rounded
collision capsule to slide off.

Edge physics was unchanged in this build. This describes OpenClaw's behavior,
without proving original-game parity. The brief falling animation at the first
real ladder still needed reproduction. Report: `ladder-idle-edges-audit`.

**0.4.11 follow-up:** after the human clarified “top and jump”, persistent grounded
fall animation was reproduced/fixed. Residual float velocities and slow corner
position corrections exceeded an overly strict animation tolerance. Physical
motion was unchanged. [Details, failures and twelve-jump regression](FPS-0.4.11.md).

## Officer on a raised step

Trials used an isolated 48 px step, ordinary offensive AI and an unprotected hero,
not the actual campaign scene. Setup assigned ammo but no shots were fired.

Claw escaped the live Officer with a jump on both sides and defeated him by
sword. Maximum rise while receiving damage: 72.3 px on the right, 92.5 px on the
left, distinct from an uninterrupted full jump of about 155 px.

An earlier left-side trial ended in death: a short directional pulse fell within
the hurt animation, so Claw did not turn. The test was corrected to hold direction
through recovery; production combat was unchanged. Retained failure:
`stairs-48-live-escape-left`; passes: `stairs-48-live-escape`,
`stairs-48-facing-held-left`.

The code prevents starting an attack during hurt animation. The inspected DAMAGE
animation lasts 350 ms, post-hit protection 500 ms; observed Officer hits were
about 530 ms apart. The small response window warrants further audit, but does
not prove faulty hitboxes or original parity. No hitbox expansion/enemy nerf.

## Cyclic disappearing platforms

The human confirmed periodic disappearance, rather than crumbling underfoot.
Actual level-one platform centers: (4651,4034) → (4545,3971) → (4453,3902).
Height gaps: 63 and 69 px; horizontal gaps: 106 and 92 px.

`tools/gameplay_perf_smoke.py --level 1 --seconds 20 --observe --cyclic-pegs`
passed both transfers; ordinary full jump rose 155 px. Each jump began only after
confirmed ground contact at least 200 ms after teleporting, with no catnip,
protection or ammo change. The tool waits for the target's active phase and
schedules app-local input, proving reachability rather than human ease.
Report: `cyclic-pegs-grounded-start`.

OpenClaw disables collision on frame 1→2 and enables it on 2→1. The retracting
image remains partly visible after support stops. Neither cycle nor jump reach
changed; not every platform or Windows timing was checked.

The first helper asserted in Box2D by querying an inactive platform's bounds.
Only diagnostics changed: read/cache bounds in the active phase. The second
helper also rejected stale post-teleport ground flags. The final report requires
real contact before every jump. These fixture failures are not ordinary-game bugs.
