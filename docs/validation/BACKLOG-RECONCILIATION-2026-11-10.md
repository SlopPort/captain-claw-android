## Finding

The backlog mixed **completed implementations**, **missing scenario coverage**, **original-behavior research** and **one final release run**. Broad unchecked headings made finished work look unimplemented. Some repeated fixtures gathered newer-artifact evidence without a relevant production change; those repetitions were not new fixes and should not have been the default next step. Keeping results version-specific does not require redoing every completed check after every version-number, documentation or diagnostic-only edit.

The November 10 hurt-voice correction was a real separate defect: debug42 accepted the generic impact but omitted the configured Soldier voice; debug43 passed the baseline/fixed comparison. It was not another implementation of the earlier gain or Catnip-loading fixes. The recent mid-ladder damage/death cases also filled a previously missing state combination; they were not another cap or pause retest.

## Work that remains genuinely open

- Natural stair combat and specific unresolved original enemy/boss decisions; no blanket health/speed/hitbox rewrite.
- Remaining picker, device-change, display, touch and lifecycle combinations listed in TODO after subtracting completed cases.

- One final release-candidate build, selected/full acceptance checks, signed upgrade/runtime evidence, notices and publication. Do not perform this final gate repeatedly on every development version.

## Rule for the next worker

Read the completed-scope column before selecting a task. Record the **uncovered scenario or relevant changed production path** before re-running a fixture. A new bug report and the frozen release candidate are also valid triggers. A newer version number, edited TODO, added diagnostic command or historical report saying “not all cases” is not sufficient alone. Retain original hashes and limitations; reusing evidence for prioritization does not relabel it as runtime-tested on a newer APK. Do not invent broader acceptance conditions merely to keep an otherwise completed task open.
